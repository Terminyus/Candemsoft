Orpigo

Read faster. Remember more.

A speed-reading app built on the ORP (Optimal Recognition Point) technique. Upload a PDF, pick your speed, and the text flows word by word at your focal point. Designed for reading, and nothing else.

Services

What we do, and how.

We work in five areas. Most projects touch several: an e-commerce site is also a design job, and often a mobile app.

  1. 01

    Web development

    Company websites, e-commerce and custom web apps. Fast to load, easy to find on search engines, easy to manage.

  2. 02

    Mobile apps

    Apps for iOS and Android. We ship client apps with the same process we use to put our own products on the App Store.

  3. 03

    AI

    Adding AI features to products: text generation, classification, assistants. Where it actually saves time, not for show.

  4. 04

    UI/UX design

    We settle flows and screens before writing code. Fewer surprises during development, cheaper changes.

  5. 05

    Consulting

    Start here if you're not sure what to build. Technology choices, project planning, reviewing an existing system.
