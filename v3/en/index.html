Lead story

Four apps live, a fifth on the way.

Istanbul software team Candemsoft ships its own products to the App Store and Google Play alongside its client work.

Candemsoft · Istanbul

Seyyah. A social discovery app for people who actually go places. Pick a spot on the map, go there in person, GPS verifies you, and your check-in becomes a memory.

Orpigo. A speed-reading app built on the ORP (Optimal Recognition Point) technique. Upload a PDF, pick your speed, and the text flows word by word at your focal point. Designed for reading, and nothing else.

Kişisel QR. A digital business card behind a QR code. Contact details, social accounts and vehicle info live in one profile; changes go live instantly and you choose who sees what.

CandemFit. A social fitness app that brings GPS workout tracking, animated route maps, story sharing, badges, a weekly league and mood, step and sleep tracking together in a warm, non-judgmental experience.
