**Short answer:** ORP (Optimal Recognition Point) is the letter in a word where your eye recognizes it fastest. [Orpigo](https://orpigo.com/) aligns each word on that point and shows words one at a time in the center of the screen, so your eyes don't travel across lines. Upload a PDF, pick a speed and start reading.

## What is the ORP technique?

When you read a word your eye usually lands on a single point near its beginning and your brain fills in the rest. RSVP (rapid serial visual presentation) apps fix that point in one place: each new word appears at the same spot with the focus letter highlighted. No more jumping to the next line.
