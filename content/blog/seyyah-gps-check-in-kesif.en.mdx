**Short answer:** [Seyyah](https://seyyah.info/) is a social discovery app for people who actually go places. Pick a place on the map, go there, GPS confirms you are there and your check-in becomes a memory. It covers all 81 provinces of Türkiye.

## Why GPS-verified check-ins?

You can only check in by being at the place, so the recommendations come from people who really went.

## Features

- **GPS-verified check-ins**
- **Collectible tags**
- **Daily streaks**
- **Discover hidden places**
- **Social discovery through people you trust**
- **All 81 provinces of Türkiye**
