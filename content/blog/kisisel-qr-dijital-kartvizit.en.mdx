**Short answer:** [Kişisel QR](https://kisiselqr.com/) is a digital business card app: your contact details, social accounts and vehicle info live in one profile shared through a QR code. Update the profile and everyone sees the new version instantly. You choose who sees what.

## Features

- **Unlimited sharing**
- **Instant updates:** no reprinting when details change
- **Visibility control:** choose which information is visible
- **Theme selection**
- **Instant notifications**
- **QR stickers and a corporate package**

## Car window QR sticker

A Kişisel QR sticker can go on your car window. Another driver or a passer-by can scan it and send you a notification such as "your window is open" or "your headlights are on", so there is no need to look up plates or leave notes.
