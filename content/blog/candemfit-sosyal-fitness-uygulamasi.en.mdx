**Short answer:** [CandemFit](https://candemfit.com/) is a social fitness app that combines GPS workout tracking with social features and wellness tracking in a warm, non-judgmental experience. Record your run or walk, watch your route on an animated map, earn badges and compete in a weekly league.

## Features

- **GPS workout tracking**
- **Animated route maps**
- **Story sharing**
- **Badges and challenges**
- **Weekly league**
- **Mood, step and sleep tracking**

## Platforms

iOS app and website available; Android coming soon. See [candemfit.com](https://candemfit.com/).
