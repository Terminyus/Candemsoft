~/candemsoft/apps

Our own products.

Four apps in the stores, one on the way. Tilt the cards with your mouse.

Seyyah

platforms: [ios, android, web]

Discover Türkiye. Every check-in is a memory.

A social discovery app for people who actually go places. Pick a spot on the map, go there in person, GPS verifies you, and your check-in becomes a memory.

App Store ↗Google Play ↗Web ↗

Orpigo

platforms: [ios, android, web]

Read faster. Remember more.

A speed-reading app built on the ORP (Optimal Recognition Point) technique. Upload a PDF, pick your speed, and the text flows word by word at your focal point. Designed for reading, and nothing else.

App Store ↗Google Play ↗Web ↗

Kişisel QR

platforms: [ios, android, web]

Take your digital identity forward.

A digital business card behind a QR code. Contact details, social accounts and vehicle info live in one profile; changes go live instantly and you choose who sees what.

App Store ↗Google Play ↗Web ↗

CandemFit

platforms: [ios, web]

Move. Share. Get stronger together.

A social fitness app that brings GPS workout tracking, animated route maps, story sharing, badges, a weekly league and mood, step and sleep tracking together in a warm, non-judgmental experience.
