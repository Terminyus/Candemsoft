Discover Türkiye. Every check-in is a memory.

  • + Collectible stickers
  • + Daily streaks

Orpigo

platforms: [ios, android, web]

Read faster. Remember more.

A speed-reading app built on the ORP (Optimal Recognition Point) technique. Upload a PDF, pick your speed, and the text flows word by word at your focal point. Designed for reading, and nothing else.

  • + Upload a PDF, it's processed automatically
  • + Adjustable reading speed
  • + Pick up where you left off
  • + All your PDFs in one place
  • + Free and Premium plans
